# 🛡️ Thread Count Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 21, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 21, 2026  

Welcome to **Thread Count** ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and financial data of our users ("you" or "user"). This Privacy Policy explains how Thread Count collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our web application, desktop utilities, and mobile integrations (collectively, the "Service").

---

## 1. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Thread Count's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.**

Specifically:
- We only request access to Google scopes strictly necessary for the operation of Thread Count (`spreadsheets`, `drive.file`, `openid`, `userinfo.email`, and `userinfo.profile`).
- We do **NOT** use Google user data to serve advertisements, build consumer profiles, or sell data to third-party data brokers.
- We do **NOT** use data accessed via Google APIs to train generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
- Human review of your Google data is strictly prohibited unless you provide affirmative consent for technical debugging or where required by law.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
1. **Google Account Information**: When you choose to sign in via Google OAuth 2.0, we receive your primary email address, full name, and profile picture URL.
2. **Google Spreadsheet Identifiers**: The Google Spreadsheet ID or URL you choose to link for tracking your Vinted sales, lots, and shipping expenses.
3. **User-Submitted Feedback & Support Requests**: When you submit suggestions, feature requests, or bug reports via our sidebar feedback tool, we record the category, star rating, your message, and timestamp.
4. **Vinted Order & Closet HTML Data**: When you import sales history or active closet listings, Thread Count parses order items, prices, buyer fees, shipping status, and item URLs locally in memory.

### B. Automatically Collected Telemetry & Diagnostics
1. **Application Usage Events**: Aggregated counts of feature interactions (e.g., batch imports executed, inventory lots created, shipping cost calculations).
2. **Runtime Error Logs**: Exception type, sanitized error message, page location, and technical stack traces to alert our administrative engineering team to bugs.
3. **Session Timestamps**: Login timestamp and authentication method.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:
- Authenticate your identity and provide secure session access.
- Read, calculate, and append your Vinted inventory, orders, and expenses directly into your designated Google Sheet.
- Compute gross revenue, realized net profit, cost-of-goods-sold (COGS), platform fees, and packaging costs.
- Triage, investigate, and fix application bugs and software exceptions.
- Prioritize product roadmap features based on user ratings and feedback submissions.

---

## 4. How Your Data Is Stored & Protected

1. **User Spreadsheet Data Stays in Your Google Drive**:
   - Thread Count operates on a decentralized data architecture. Your inventory records, sales transactions, and financial analytics remain securely stored inside **your own personal Google Drive account**. We do not maintain a duplicate external database of your private sales data.
2. **OAuth Tokens**:
   - Access tokens and authorization credentials granted via Google OAuth are kept securely in encrypted session memory and are never exposed to other users or external services.
3. **Local Telemetry & Feedback**:
   - Operational error logs and user feedback are stored in a local encrypted database and synced to a secure administrative Google Sheet accessible exclusively to authorized Thread Count administrators.

## 5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do **NOT** sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or Google data under any circumstances. We may disclose information only in the following limited situations:
- **With Your Explicit Consent**: If you ask our support team to inspect a specific error log or spreadsheet format issue.
- **Service Providers**: Third-party hosting providers (e.g., Streamlit Cloud, Google Cloud) that facilitate server hosting and API connectivity under strict confidentiality obligations.
- **Legal Compliance**: When required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

## 6. Data Retention & Your Rights

- **Google Permissions Revocation**: You can instantly disconnect Thread Count from accessing your Google Account at any time by visiting your [Google Account Permissions Dashboard](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
- **Data Deletion**: You can request full deletion of your user profile, feedback submissions, and error logs from our administrative systems by emailing [privacy@threadcount.com](mailto:privacy@threadcount.com) or [nicashlod@gmail.com](mailto:nicashlod@gmail.com). We will process and confirm your request within 7 business days.
- **Data Portability**: Because your data lives in standard Google Sheets, your data is 100% portable and accessible to you at any time, even if you stop using Thread Count.
